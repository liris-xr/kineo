For each camera, the error is its estimated offset to the reference camera minus the frame-quantized ground-truth offset, reported in milliseconds. The raw AIST++ recordings (about 21\,s) open with a metronome count-in; \emph{Count-in removed} keeps only the music (about 11\,s) and serves as the baseline for the other conditions. \emph{+ Periodic beep} inserts a 1\,kHz, 50\,ms beep every 0.5\,s, at the same instants in every camera's audio. \emph{+ Park ambient} and \emph{+ Cafeteria babble} mix a real recording from the DEMAND dataset~\cite{thiemann2013demand} into every camera at the indicated signal-to-noise ratio (SNR); each camera receives a different excerpt, so the noise is uncorrelated across cameras and cannot itself be leveraged by the method. The beep alone is ambiguous up to its 0.5\,s period, yet no camera locks onto an adjacent beep: the music underneath disambiguates it, and the beep then sharpens offset estimation, nearly recovering the accuracy of the raw recordings with the count-in. Accuracy degrades as the SNR decreases (i.e., as the distractor gets louder), more so for babble than for ambient noise, and markedly at 0\,dB. Finally, the mean signed error being positive in every condition shows that our estimated offsets are on average late with respect to the ground truth.}
